How to Trade

Your complete Bamik guide

1
The Basics

Bamik is a binary options platform. You place a small stake on a short prediction (1–5 seconds). If your prediction is right, you receive your stake back plus a fixed payout. If wrong, you lose only the stake — never more.

2
Demo vs Real

Switch any time using the account pill at the top.

  • Demo — practice with virtual funds. No real money at risk.
  • Real — uses your deposited balance. Wins/losses are real.

3
Markets — Volatility Indices

We offer synthetic Volatility Indices (Vol 10, 25, 50, 75, 100, 1T). They run 24/7 and aren't affected by news or market hours — pure price action. Higher Vol = bigger swings = bigger risk and reward.

4
Contract Types — Deep Dive

Even / Odd~95%

Predict whether the last digit of the price will be even or odd. Payout is ~95% of your stake.

Over / Under~90-100%

Predict if the next tick will be above or below a specific digit threshold. Higher risk, higher reward.

Match / Differ~950%

Predict if the last digit will match your chosen digit. High payout, lower probability.
