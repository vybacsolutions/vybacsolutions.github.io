FAQs

Everything you need to know before taking your first task.

How do payouts work?

Once the validator approves your task, the reward is released from the task payout schedule into your wallet or withdrawal account.

Why do I need a package?

Packages unlock access to tasks and define your account capability, payout multiplier, and withdrawal threshold.

How is the deposit amount calculated?

The system multiplies your selected package value by the live USD-to-KES rate so the amount always reflects the current market price.
