STRATEGY SUITE

Six layouts. One brain. One decision.

Every fresh sequence, the engine evaluates multiple dual-and-triple digit layouts against the live tick distribution, then fires only the one with the highest empirical edge. No fixed pair. No guessing.

CORE
Dual Edge

Classic two-sided entry. Balanced coverage on both tails of the digit distribution.

BALANCED
Wide Coverage

Zero dead-zone configuration. Every digit is covered by at least one leg. Lower payout, higher hit-rate.

TRIPLE
Favour Over

Three-leg layout weighted to the upper tail. Auto-selected when the recent tick stream skews high.

TRIPLE
Favour Under

Mirror of Favour Over. Weighted to the lower tail. Selected when the stream skews low.

AGGRESSIVE
High-Prob Sniper

Three-leg layout tuned for maximum probability per entry. Lower payout per leg, but statistically the safest shape we run.

RECOVERY
Adaptive Recovery

When the primary sequence stalls, the engine shifts to a recovery layout with a tuned stake ladder to climb back.

🛡️
Why only on this terminal

The layout-selection model, payout-gating logic, phase-switching thresholds, and stake-ladder are engine-side only. They are not visible to the browser and are not documented anywhere on this site. You get the terminal — the reasoning stays with us.

Questions

Do I need my own Deriv account?+

Yes. You connect your own Deriv account via OAuth or API token. The engine runs on your account, with your stake, under your control.

How do I pay?+

TRC20 USDT only. You can send from Binance, from Deriv's USDT wallet, from any exchange, or from a personal wallet. The exact address and QR appear after you click Pay.

Can I send USDT from Deriv directly?+

Yes. Cashier → Withdraw → USDT → TRC20 network → paste our address → 10 USDT. Same goes for Binance or any exchange. Make sure the network is TRC20 (Tron).

How long does activation take?+

After you paste the transaction hash, activation is usually completed within minutes during business hours. You will see a confirmation in the panel.

Can I lose money?+

Yes. This is a trading terminal, not a guarantee. Trade only with what you can afford to lose. Start on demo.

What if I send USDT on the wrong network?+

Funds sent on any network other than TRC20 are lost and cannot be recovered. Double-check the network before sending.
