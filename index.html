About & Risk Disclosure

WHAT THIS IS

PURE MATCHES AI is an experimental statistical analysis tool for Deriv digit-based synthetic indices. It collects live tick data, extracts the last digit of each quote, and evaluates all ten digits across six statistical features over five rolling windows. A composite score is produced for each digit. The highest-scoring digit becomes the candidate — but only if it clears every configured threshold and the risk engine approves execution.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

This is not a guaranteed-profit system. It is not financial advice. It does not predict the future. Deriv synthetic indices are generated by a cryptographically secure random number generator — historical digit frequencies do not influence future outcomes. Any apparent statistical bias is transient noise. The strategy engine exists to filter weak candidates and control risk exposure, not to produce a mathematical edge.

RISK DISCLOSURE

  • Trading involves financial risk. You can lose money.
  • Past performance does not guarantee future results.
  • No profit, income or accuracy is promised or implied.
  • Paper mode simulates contracts — no real orders are placed.
  • Real mode requires explicit confirmation and a valid Deriv API token.
  • Never trade with funds you cannot afford to lose.
  • Fixed staking is enforced. No martingale. No recovery multiplier.
  • Session limits (daily loss, consecutive losses, max trades) lock the bot when breached.
